About Us

Our Services

Myers Underground Utilities, LLC is a Mississippi based contractor serving clients from both the public and private sector. The firm is licensed, insured and bonded in the state of Mississippi and has completed projects in Mississippi as well as Alabama. Myers Underground has extensive experience in the construction of infrastructure for roads, malls, subdivisions and numerous public works projects including water and sanitary sewer systems, sewage lagoons, water treatment plants, storm drainage and pumping stations. 


Peyton Myers, owner and third generation contractor, has built the company on a strong foundation of safety, quality, and service. 


Our employees are our most important resource and we're committed to their safety. With a safety program modeled after some of the best in the industry, Myers Underground has an outstanding record with no lost days or lost time due to accidents or injury. 


Myers Underground has a wide range of capabilities, but while the nature of our projects may vary, our primary objective is always the same: deliver high quality professional services that are result oriented. This has provided Myers Underground with the reputation for excellence that our clients have come to expect.
