About us

Experience you can trust.

Myers Underground Utilities, LLC is a Mississippi-based contractor serving clients in the public and private sectors. The firm is licensed, insured, and bonded in Mississippi and has completed projects in Mississippi and Alabama.

We bring extensive experience in infrastructure for roads, malls, subdivisions, and public works — including water and sanitary sewer systems, sewage lagoons, water treatment plants, storm drainage, and pumping stations.

Peyton Myers, owner and third-generation contractor, has built the company on safety, quality, and service. Our employees are our most important resource; our safety program is modeled after industry leaders, with an outstanding record.

While projects vary, our objective stays the same: deliver high-quality, result-oriented professional services — the reputation our clients expect.
