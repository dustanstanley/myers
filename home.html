About us

Experience you can trust.

Myers Underground Utilities, LLC is a Mississippi-based contractor serving the public and private sectors. We are licensed, insured, and bonded in Mississippi with completed work in Mississippi and Alabama.

We deliver infrastructure for roads, malls, subdivisions, and public works — water and sanitary sewer systems, sewage lagoons, water treatment plants, storm drainage, and pumping stations.

Peyton Myers, owner and third-generation contractor, built the company on safety, quality, and service. Our employees are our most important resource; our safety program follows industry best practices. While every project differs, our goal is the same: high-quality, result-oriented service — the standard our clients expect.
